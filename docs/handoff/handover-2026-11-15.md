## 10. Humidity noise statistics, 16 November 2026 — not shipped

Branch `fix/humidity-noise-statistic`, base `main` at `4d30778`. Production code was not changed. Humidity stays on normalised second-difference variance at calibrated ratio 30. Temperature was not retuned.

Windows are the accuracy-gate construction: `CSVAdapter` physical units, sequence length 20, stride 6, first half calibrates, second half is the eval set. One flag per window, the same question `NoiseDetector.detect` answers. Quiet recall is 20 copies of that gate's ideal window (seeds 0–19; humidity already carries 0.08 %RH) plus an independent Gaussian on the humidity column. The noise generator is `default_rng(80000 + seed + round(sigma * 1000))`.

The threshold rule was fixed before the run, and it was not swept. Flag when statistic / baseline > 30. Baseline is the calibration-half median. The production statistic also applies `max(median, 0.04)`; on July the median is 0.213, so the floor does not bind. The reimplementation of second-difference variance matched `NoiseDetector.detect` on every cohort, including the quiet σ = 2 windows.

Four statistics, humidity only:

1. **d2_var** — high-pass, then `var(diff(hp, 2)) / 6`. This is production.
2. **hp_var** — variance of that same high-pass residual over the 20 samples, no second difference.
3. **mad_var** — (MAD of the high-pass residual / Φ⁻¹(0.75))², so it estimates a variance.
4. **hf_band** — periodogram power above 0.25 cycles/sample (the top octave, above the Butterworth cutoff). Band energy, not a power fraction: a fraction does not move when the amplitude goes from 1 %RH to 3 %RH.

### 11b. Single-sensor bias

`BIAS_EMA_SPAN` is 288. A pipeline window is `SEQ_LEN` 20, so the EMA check never votes. The midpoint `BiasOffsetDetector` is not a fix. One helper was measured and not written into the tree, and it was not wired into the pipeline.

The rule compares the current 20 samples with the previous 20 (the persisted portion). Slope is the median first difference of the reference. The center is the median of its last 5 samples. Expected value at step i is that center plus slope × i. Flag when the median residual is beyond 2.0 °C (the temperature EMA threshold) and at least 80% of the window is beyond 2.0 °C on that same side. A smooth ramp continues the slope and does not flag; a level step does. Threshold and fraction were not swept after these counts.

July fixture, greenhouse temperature, every window that has 20 samples of history. A full-window fault adds +3 °C to all 20 samples. A half-window fault adds +3 °C to the second half only.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 (all) | 330 | 6 (1.8%) | 242 (73%) | 5 (1.5%) |
| 6 (second half) | 165 | 2 (1.2%) | 123 (74%) | 2 (1.2%) |

Clean flags are under about 2%. The full-window step is about 73–75%, short of about 80%. The half-window step is about 1%, because the rule asks the offset to cover most of the window. A raw median against the previous window, with no slope, is not viable either: the clean median move over 20 samples has p50 1.5 °C and the same 2.0 °C cut flags about 22% of stride-6 windows.
